Barnaby · the film · v2.1

Script v2.1 · your notes folded in

A product film about a family, two desks and the systems they run on, with Barnaby arriving as the newest member of the household and being something different to each of them. Three minutes.

World bible: video/v2/WORLD.md. Look book (gate 2) in progress: the shop desk diorama, the shop PC (done), Theo's Lisbon desk, and Barnaby's three worlds (being rolled). Then an animatic (gate 3) before any full render.

The script

1. INT. SHOP BACK OFFICE — THURSDAY NIGHT Nine tabs0:00 – 0:12

Black. Rain on a window. A PC fan spins up; a hard drive chatters. Light comes up on the back office: one desk, a desk lamp, a 2009 monitor. We drift toward it.

On the screen, browser tabs open one after another. The memory gadget's needle climbs into the red.

Martin (o.s., from the workshop, tired)~170 wpm

Theo — where's the parts list again?

Theo~185 wpm

Tab four. No — five.

(beat)

It's the one with the spanner.

Picture

Diorama wide → push to the monitor. The shop PC: nine tabs, the parts list's spanner icon, the memory dial in the red (look book: shop-pc-03).

Sound

Rain, PC fan, HDD clicks, a radio far off in the workshop. Tabs open with small real clicks. No music yet.

2. INT. SHOP BACK OFFICE — NIGHT (CONT'D) The handover0:12 – 0:40

Across the desk: the 2009 photo of teenage Theo and Martin outside the shop, Theo holding a CD-R marked SHOP SYSTEM v1. The wall calendar, Friday circled. A moving box: THEO — LISBON.

Theo (quietly)

Seventeen years.

He closes the tabs, one by one. A brass bell rings softly: Barnaby arrives on the old PC.

Theo

Right. Barnaby — this is the shop's system. I wrote it when I was fourteen, so be kind. Look after them for me.

Theo's view: Synapse reading his code, the real output scrolling past. Twenty-three endpoints; six light up.

Barnaby~155 wpm

Twenty-three endpoints, sir. I've taken the six that only read — nobody's given me any keys yet.

Theo

It's Theo. And don't call my uncle sir — he'll unplug you.

Barnaby

Noted.

Theo slides one key off his own ring, a paper tag on it, BACK OFFICE, and leaves it on the keyboard. The lamp clicks off.

Picture

Desk detail shots (the props say who Theo is), Barnaby on the 2009 monitor in Theo's world, modest on old hardware. The key on the keyboard is the last shot.

Synapse (diegetic)

On Theo's screen: synapse build --auto --local · Extracted 23 endpoint(s) · 0.5 s

Sound

The real brass bell (Barnaby's sound for the whole film). Music enters: a sparse, warm acoustic guitar. The key on the keyboard: a small, clean metal tap.

3. INT. SHOP BACK OFFICE — MONDAY MORNING Monday0:40 – 0:56

Time-lapse: daylight sweeps across the desk. Theo's things are gone. A hand takes the key from the keyboard. Tea goes into the WORLD'S OKAYEST NEPHEW mug. Reading glasses, a greasy rag, a paper job card. The screen clock: 08:52.

Martin (sitting, a sigh)

Right. Barnaby, is it?

Barnaby

Good morning, Martin.

Martin

What's due tomorrow — and are we short on anything?

Martin's view: the shop building as a floor plan. Barnaby walks the workshop floor, then the stockroom.

Barnaby

Five bikes due tomorrow. Only Anika's is a problem — her brakes need two pairs of pads, and we've one left.

On the shelf: a parts box. One pair of pads inside.

Picture

Same desk, new owner: the props swap. Barnaby on the shop PC in Martin's world.

Sound

Workshop morning: radio, a freewheel ticking, a compressor. Music: light, walking pace.

Payoff

"Good morning, Martin." lands Theo's line.

4. INT. SHOP BACK OFFICE — DAY A key0:56 – 1:13

Martin

Order ten from Madison, then.

Barnaby

I can't order parts myself yet — but Theo built the system a way to. Shall I take that on?

A card: Let Barnaby order parts from your suppliers. On the plan, the back door where deliveries come in shows a small key.

Martin

Go on, then.

He clicks. A key turns; the back door unlocks on the plan.

Barnaby

Ten pairs from Madison, at six pounds fifty a pair. Shall I?

Martin

Yes.

Theo's Purchase Orders page: order 3 slides in. Brake pads (Shimano resin, pair) · 10 · Madison · ordered.

Engine note

Sound

5. INT. SHOP BACK OFFICE — AFTERNOON The same four steps1:13 – 1:35

Light slides across the desk. Through the doorway, a finished bike comes off the repair stand: the clamp releases with a clunk.

Martin

Barnaby — Sofia's bike's done.

Barnaby

I'll need to text her and mark it ready. May I?

Two quick cards: Let Barnaby text customers. Let Barnaby mark bikes ready.

Martin (clicking both)

Go on.

Barnaby walks four rooms: the job, the customer, a text, the status. Another bike comes off the stand.

Martin

Kwame's.

The same four rooms, faster. The clunk again.

Martin

And Jana's.

Barnaby

That's three bikes handed back, and the same four steps each time. Shall I make it one?

Can you?

Barnaby

Give me a moment.

On the plan, a passage opens through the four rooms: Hand back a bike.

Martin

Tom's Brompton.

Barnaby

Tom's been texted, and it's marked ready.

Engine note

Real

6. INT. SHOP BACK OFFICE — LATE AFTERNOON A room Theo never built1:35 – 2:08

Martin

Who's got too much on this week?

Barnaby

The system can't tell me — there's no report for it. Theo always meant to write one.

On the bezel, the one sticky note: "Reports: coming soon (sorry Uncle M)". On screen, the tab: Reports (coming soon!!).

Barnaby

I could write it, if you like.

Martin

You can do that?

Barnaby

With your say-so. I'll work on a copy — nothing changes until you've seen it.

The monitor fills with code, written line by line; it reflects on the glossy panel. Martin's glasses rest beside the keyboard. A row of checks turns green.

Barnaby

Done. It adds up each mechanic's hours for the week, the way Theo's notes count them. It only reads — it can't change a thing — and all of Theo's checks still pass. Shall I add it?

Martin

Add it.

Barnaby

That one needs your click, Martin. I don't change the system on a word.

Martin (clicking)

Fussy.

Theo's system reloads. The tab that said "Reports (coming soon!!)" now just says Reports. On the plan, a new room appears in the back office: Workload.

Barnaby

Dev's carrying thirty-one hours against his twenty. Priya starts tomorrow — shall I give her the two wheel builds?

Martin

Good idea.

In the job list, two rows change: Dev → Priya.

Picture

The centrepiece. The explanation card in plain words (what it does, what it reads, changes nothing, checks pass, can be undone; the code behind "see the change"). The "coming soon!!" tab changing is the emotional payoff (look book: shop-pc-06 → 07).

Same butler, three worlds

A three-way split at the moment the report lands: Theo's world (the diff, tests passing), Martin's (a new room), Priya's (two new tags on her bench). Under the hood, one event.

Barnaby — new job. This is the crew's memory.

Synapse reads it in seconds. Barnaby, fully himself on this machine, in Theo's world.

Barnaby

Thirteen endpoints, sir — and two deadlines in dispute. Shall I flag them?

Theo

Still Theo.

Barnaby

Theo.

And the render box?

Barnaby

Twenty-eight endpoints. It's on the big card, and three jobs are queued.

Theo (grinning)

Show-off.
