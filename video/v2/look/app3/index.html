Barnaby, round 3

The director on round 2: too subdued, no personality, and the icon needs work. "No AI apps look like this." So this round is modelled on the Claude and Codex desktop apps, after studying both aspect by aspect (REFERENCE-ANALYSIS.md). His work now sits inline and collapsed, with one live line while he works. His words are set in a warm reading serif on an ivory (or charcoal) ground, and brass is the one brand colour. And he has a mark of his own: a counter bell wearing a bow tie.

Pending the director's approval. ?skin=app (round 2) and the old default are unchanged.

Open it: .venv/Scripts/python -m barnaby serve, then http://127.0.0.1:8700/?skin=app3 (add &mock=1 to replay the run, &turn=4 for the Build, &theme=dark for dark, &overlay=1 for the compact window).
