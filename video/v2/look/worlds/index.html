Barnaby in the film: three worlds

One butler, three people, three worlds. Under the hood it is one building, one set of 24 abilities and one story (the same data files feed every frame); on screen each person gets the world they already understand.

Barnaby is three different things to three different people, and we are trying to give them all the exact same look. … Impeccable lets you roll a dice and select a world and really leans into that world as a medium of design.

How each round ran: seven grounded worlds from that person’s own culture, written down before the dice; impeccable concept-seed --scope direction --mode experience picked which one gets built and dealt catalogue challengers; every challenger was fused with the story and weighed against the dealt world on audience identification and product clarity. Losers donate one discipline each, shown as named raises on the dealt world. Pick a card per person, or ask for a re-roll.
