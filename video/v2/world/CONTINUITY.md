## The parts cabinet agrees with Theo's parts list (shop-pc-03b)

The list says where things are kept, so the drawers carry those numbers in Martin's hand: **1 BRAKE PADS** (pulled
out: ONE pair of resin pads with their spring, in a zip bag), **2 CABLES** (gear cable sets), **3 BAR TAPE**,
**4 CHAINS / CASSETTES** (a cassette 11-32 box, two X11 chain boxes), **6 TUBES / SPOKES** (boxed 700x25c tubes and a
banded bundle of 292 mm spokes), **7 BB** (HT2 boxes and a loose BB). Drawer 5 isn't on the list: it holds **BELLS**
(counter sales). The small unnumbered drawers are M5 BOLTS, SPOKE NIPPLES, BEARINGS and VALVE CORES. The wall shelf is
labelled **SHELF A** and holds the spare headset ("headset: shelf A"). Shelf B (brake fluid, tyres) and the cupboard
(rims) are in the workshop, not on this set. On Tuesday morning, drawer 1 still has one pair: Monday's Madison order
(10 pairs, next-day delivery) hasn't arrived by 08:34.

## Cables (nothing ends in space)

Shop: keyboard (purple PS/2), mouse (USB), speakers (audio to the green jack, USB power) and VGA run behind the monitor
to the tower's rear I/O. Mains (tower, monitor, lamp) drop down the 2 cm gap between the desk and the wall (the desk
stands against the skirting) to a four-way strip on the floor, which plugs into a double socket under the desk. The
headphones plug into the tower's front jack. The workshop work lamp runs down its arm to a socket behind the bench. The
radio is battery-powered. Lisbon: both monitors' DisplayPort and power run down the arm into the grommet and the tray
under the desk; the laptop's USB-C goes to the dock; the keyboard (orange coiled lead) and the tablet go into the dock;
the dock goes to the grommet; the tray's strip goes to the wall socket; the mouse is wireless (receiver in the dock).
